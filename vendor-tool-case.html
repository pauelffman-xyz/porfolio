Context & Problem
Vendors were running restaurants
through phone calls and spreadsheets.

"Our previous vendor platform faced two main challenges: management friction and an operating ceiling." Every new branch added more manual overhead instead of less — the tool wasn't built to scale with the network.

Fragmentation & latency (Q4)
Many branches were still relying on the least efficient order-reception method available — with acceptance times stretching past 7 minutes per order. A meaningful share of branches had no digital tool at all, running purely on phone calls.
Service volatility (Q3)
Even where the tool was adopted, performance was inconsistent — degrading sharply during peak hours and specific high-demand months. Service Level dropped noticeably in a single month alone. Vendors needed stability, not just a tool.

Task & Vision
Three pillars.
One self-managed vendor.

"Our task was to design a solution that scales operational efficiency and eliminates manual dependencies to transform our vendors into strategic partners." That translated into three pillars — our Vision.

Pillar 01
Self-Management
Vendors control their own menu, photos, prices, orders and team — without calling support for every change.
Pillar 02
Optimization with Data / AI

Pillar 03
Ecosystem Growth
Cross-sell touchpoints (financial services, photography tools) turn the dashboard into a growth channel, not just an admin panel.
The Solution · Vendor Tool
One dashboard for products, orders, status and finances.

I designed the new management dashboard end-to-end: catalog and menu, real-time order management, operational status, franchise-level rollups and billing — for both single-branch owners and multi-branch franchises.

Franchise-wide view

Optimization with AI · Photo Governance
"2 of 3 customers decide
based on the photo."

Menu photography was the single biggest lever on conversion — and the hardest thing to standardize across 545 independent vendors. I designed a lightweight in-flow photography guide plus an automated review pipeline, so quality control doesn't require a human reviewer on every upload.

In-flow photography tips
Framing & composition
Rule-of-thirds guide overlaid directly on example shots.
Natural lighting
Simple, non-technical language — no photography background required.
Tempting framing
Coaches vendors to shoot fresh, colorful ingredients close-up.

Slow acceptance times were the core driver of service volatility. The new order manager surfaces every pending order with a visible countdown, batch actions, and one-tap accept/reject — designed to be usable by a busy cashier, not just an admin.

Self-Management · Permissions & Roles
One franchise,
many trusted hands.

Franchises needed to delegate day-to-day operations across branches without losing control. I designed a scoped roles system — franchise owners assign access per branch and per role, with destructive actions (like removing a user) requiring explicit confirmation.

Step 01
Migration & Standardization
Migrated the network onto the new system — with a handful of legacy Multicomercio franchises still transitioning at time of writing. This alone standardized operations across branches that previously ran on completely different tools.

Self-Management (Better UX)
Improved UI tooltips and AI-support integration to reduce the need for manual contact. The goal: make the tool simple enough to drive self-service adoption, with the operational efficiency of a POS system.
