SukuPay is a remittance platform that lets users in the US send money to Guatemala using only a phone number. Home is where users decide whether to send money, compare rates, and build trust.

The current version introduced repeat transfer, FX visibility and promotions — but still presents opportunities to improve clarity, hierarchy and conversion.

My goal was to evaluate how effectively Home supports fast, trust-driven repeat transfers, and redesign it to improve clarity, confidence and conversion while balancing growth needs.

Result · Two Directions Explored
While challenging the current Home experience
I explored two possible paths
"Minimal" proposal
Strengthen what already works.

I chose to respect the existing Home structure while refining key areas for clarity and usability. Improvements focused on iconography, hierarchy and visual consistency to reduce cognitive load and strengthen the main conversion path.

I elevated the "Resend" feature, bringing it forward as a primary element on Home to better support one of the most frequent user behaviors — repeat transfers — directly supporting the goal of reaching 100M transactions by 2026.

"The North" proposal
Scale into a financial ecosystem.

A North Star approach — evolving the product to solve more user needs over time, positioning SukuPay as a long-term financial ally rather than just a transactional tool. Brings forward the FX component, enables more prominent repeat sending, and introduces a bottom navigation with room for crypto transactions, investment options, a marketplace and a lightweight FAQ section.

Result · Full Home Proposal
Main box: Send money + secondary actions
Resend · Exchange rate · FAQs
An interface that adapts to the user's stage.

New users or without funds: the priority is funding the account, so the primary action shifts to "Add money" — supporting a successful first transaction as fast as possible.

Returning users: a Resend card reinforces repeat behavior. As users need sufficient balance to complete the transfer, this moment can seamlessly guide them to add funds and continue sending.

Expected Impact
Reflection 01
Repetition is the retention lever
In remittances, the second and third transfer matter more than the first. Designing for "Send again" directly serves the 100M transaction goal.
Reflection 02
Transparency removes support burden
Surfacing FX rate and fees on Home — instead of relying on WhatsApp — turns a support cost into a self-serve trust signal.
The core principle
"Simplicity and transparency are the foundation of trust in remittances."
Every improvement — from the sticky header to the FAQ accordion — exists to answer one question faster: is my money safe, and how much will arrive?
