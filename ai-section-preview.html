AI in my workflow

AI as a force multiplier.

AI is deeply integrated into my design workflow — not as a replacement for design thinking, but as a force multiplier that lets me spend more time on strategy, user experience and cross-functional collaboration.

My primary tool is Claude, which I use across the whole product design lifecycle. A few of the ways:

claude · design-workflow
01 · Research & benchmarking
Map the landscape, fast.

Analyze competitors, surface UX patterns, summarize findings and compare design approaches across products — turning hours of manual research into minutes.

▸
02 · Rapid prototyping
Idea to clickable, in minutes.

Build functional prototypes using my Design System, GitHub repos and existing components as context — validating ideas far faster than assembling every screen by hand.

▸
03 · Design exploration
Explore wide before committing.

Brainstorm interaction patterns, challenge assumptions and explore alternative flows. I rarely take the first output — I iterate, critique and refine it with my own product thinking and design judgment.

▸
04 · UX writing
Copy options, on brand.

Reusable prompt libraries and workflows that generate multiple copy options per scenario — exploring tone and messaging while keeping the final content aligned to product and brand voice.

▸
05 · Critique & iteration
Review through real user eyes.

Custom workflows built on our user personas, usability insights and product metrics — reviewing designs from different user perspectives, catching friction points and iterating before handing off to engineering.

▸
06 · UI production
Design System → live UI.

With Claude Design I turn my Design System straight into UI explorations and interactive prototypes — cutting repetitive production work while keeping every component consistent.
