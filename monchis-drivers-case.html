Context & Problem
Our drivers were the backbone.
Their app was an afterthought.

Monchis processed 300K orders a month, but the driver-facing app hadn't been redesigned in years. Drivers managed shifts, accepted orders, and navigated deliveries through an interface that created friction at every step — costing time, money, and driver retention.

No real-time visibility
Drivers couldn't see their assigned zone, shift schedule, or earnings clearly. Every decision required guesswork.
Clunky order management
Accepting, tracking, and completing orders involved too many taps. The progress pipeline was invisible — drivers had no sense of where they were in the flow.
No route context
The map showed the zone but not the pickup-to-delivery route. Drivers relied on switching to Google Maps mid-delivery.
Shift planning was blind
Drivers couldn't see upcoming shifts, per-order pay, or km rates. They showed up without knowing what they'd earn.

Every screen was designed around the real-world delivery sequence. Here's the full journey a driver follows — from the moment a new order arrives to marking it as delivered.

New order arrives on lock screen with store name and location.
2

3
Navigate to pickup

5
Deliver
Route to client with ETA, distance, and Google Maps integration.
Feature Focus · Home & Orders
Everything at
a glance.

The home screen shows connection status, zone map with live position, and shift controls — all without scrolling. When an order comes in, it overlays the map with store name, distance, earnings, and a countdown bar.

Connected status badge
Green "Conectado" pill always visible. Drivers know their status instantly.
Zone map with live position
The map shows the assigned zone with the driver's real-time position. No more guessing where the boundaries are.
Pause & disconnect in thumb zone
"Pausar Turno" and "Desconectarme" are full-width at the bottom — reachable one-handed on a motorcycle.
Feature Focus · Order Pipeline
The order flow
made visible.

Every in-progress order shows a 4-step visual pipeline: Aceptado → Retirado → En camino → Entregado. Drivers always know exactly where they are in the delivery. The order detail reveals client info, payment method, product list, and one-tap call buttons for both client and store.

Visual status pipeline
4-step progress bar on every order card. Color-coded: green for done, red for current, grey for pending.
Earnings upfront
Gs. 13,000 per order and payment method (EFECTIVO) visible before accepting. No hidden math.
One-tap client contact
"Llamar al cliente" and "Llamar al local" — direct call buttons, no digging through menus.
Feature Focus · Navigation
From pickup to delivery.
One map, one flow.

The active delivery screen shows the full route with real-time ETA and distance. A sticky bottom bar shows the current destination and order count. "Ir a Google maps" lets drivers hand off navigation when needed — but the route context stays inside the app.

Route + ETA + distance
"Estás a 1h 02 min - 900 mts" — visible in the bottom bar without tapping anything.
Google Maps integration
One-tap "Ir a Google maps" button for turn-by-turn when needed — but the driver never loses context.
Multi-order awareness
Order count badge shows how many deliveries are active. Drivers stay oriented across batched orders.
Feature Focus · Shift Management & Profile
Shifts, earnings, and
driver identity.

The side panel gives drivers a complete view of their account: profile, ranking tier (Monchis Silver), earnings history, shift management, and support access — all organized by context. When a driver pauses for too long, a modal with a countdown warns them before auto-disconnecting.

Ranking & gamification
"Monchis Silver" badge visible in the profile. Rankings give drivers a sense of progression and loyalty.
Pause timeout modal
"Estás llegando al límite del estado pausado" with a countdown timer. Clear choice: Activarme or Desconectarme.
Structured menu
Grouped by Tu Cuenta, Turnos y Pedidos, Centro de Ayuda — not a flat list of 15 items.

Key Design Decisions
Decision 01
Dark UI for outdoor use
Drivers work in bright sunlight. A dark interface with high-contrast text and colored CTAs reduces glare and improves readability on the road.
Decision 02
Visual order pipeline
Replaced invisible state changes with a 4-step color-coded pipeline. Drivers always know where they are — Aceptado, Retirado, En camino, Entregado.
Decision 03
Thumb-zone primary actions
Accept/reject, Pausar, Desconectarme, Llegué al local, Estoy afuera — all full-width at the bottom. One-handed, on a motorcycle — safety first.
Decision 04
Earnings before accepting
"Ganancia Gs 13.000" visible on the incoming order card, plus distance. Drivers make informed decisions before committing.
Decision 05
Pause timeout with countdown
Instead of silently disconnecting paused drivers, a modal warns with a countdown. Clear choice: Activarme or Desconectarme. Respect, not punishment.
Decision 06
Structured side menu
Tu Cuenta · Turnos y Pedidos · Centro de Ayuda · Acerca de Monchis. Grouped by context, not a flat list. Ranking badge gives drivers identity.
Learning 01
Drivers are not power users
They're on a motorcycle, in the sun, with one hand. Every interaction needs to be optimized for speed and minimal cognitive load — not feature richness.
Learning 02
Transparency drives retention
When drivers can see exactly what they'll earn per order before accepting, they trust the platform. Opaque economics create distrust and churn.
Learning 03
The pipeline is the product
Making order status visible didn't just reduce support tickets — it gave drivers a sense of progress and control. Visibility is trust.
The result
"La app que te acompaña en tu jornada de delivery."
The redesigned driver app transformed a friction-filled tool into a companion that drivers could trust: transparent earnings, visible order progress, integrated navigation, and support always one tap away. Every screen was designed for the reality of delivery work — not a desk.
